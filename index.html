Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Haveo is free on the App Store. Paid features may come later, but everything described on this page is free today.
Where do I get it?
Download Haveo free on the App Store, for iPhone and iPad. Follow @haveoapp on TikTok for what's coming next.
Can I practice out loud, by talking?
Yes. Tap the mic and talk instead of typing — your phone turns your voice into text, and on iPhone that happens on the device itself. Haveo reads its replies back out loud, so you can rehearse with your ears as well as your eyes. 🎤
Is my information private?
Your events, notes and reflections are stored on your phone, and there's no account. The AI is the main part that leaves: what you type to Haveo goes to Anthropic so it can answer, a file you attach goes the same way, and a sentence read aloud goes to OpenAI for the voice. With the AI on, saving an event also sends that event once, to write the “what to expect” note. Neither company trains on it and both delete it within about 30 days. Haveo also counts which features get opened, so we can see what actually helps — a count is the name of a feature and the date, and it can't carry anything you wrote. No ads, and nothing that follows you into other apps. This website counts anonymous page views and App Store clicks so we know which guides are useful — no profile, no advertising, and nothing tied to you.
Does it work offline?
Partly. Your events, your Cheat Sheet, your notes and Boost Cards are on your phone, so you can read them with no signal — which is the part you need in the room. Anything where Haveo writes or replies needs a connection.
Is it only for tech events?
No. Pick the kind of event and Haveo prepares for that one: a conference, a meetup, a career fair, an interview, a client call, a presentation, a social gathering, or a difficult conversation you are dreading.
Where does what I write go?
To answer you, Haveo sends what you type — and any file you attach — to Anthropic, the company whose AI writes the reply. They don't use it to teach their AI. When Haveo reads a reply out loud, that one sentence goes to OpenAI, who makes the voice. The full details are in the privacy policy.
